This annex modifies the standard 2011 GMRA for transactions executed through Bond Connect Repo
(Northbound) against onshore-CNY CIBM-eligible bonds.

## 1. Definitions

* **CIBM** — China Interbank Bond Market.
* **PBoC** — People's Bank of China.
* **CCDC** — China Central Depository & Clearing Co. Ltd.
* **HKMA-CMU** — Hong Kong Monetary Authority Central Moneymarkets Unit.

## 2. Settlement

All settlements run **Northbound** through HKMA-CMU on a free-of-payment basis with the cash leg
matching at PBoC's RMB CNAPS.

## 3. Margin

Initial margin posted in CNH; variation margin daily by 11:00 HKT.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate on 5 business days' notice at the prevailing Repo Rate plus a
break-funding spread per Schedule A.
